What is Idea Kitchen?

Idea Kitchen is a weekly newsletter that turns the overwhelming world of AI into simple, bite-sized "recipes" for real life. Each issue shows curious humans how to use AI tools to save time, think more clearly, or just get something off their plate — no prior experience required. Written by Marissa Goldberg.

Most AI content out there sounds like it was written by a robot, for robots. Idea Kitchen does the opposite — a warm, practical place where curious humans come to learn one simple recipe at a time.

Making AI feel less intimidating and more human.

Who it's for

Idea Kitchen is for curious humans who want to actually use AI in their day-to-day — not just read about it. They're smart, capable, probably great at their job or creative projects or running their household. What they're not is a prompt engineer who wants to spend their Saturday reading whitepapers.

They might be running a business, managing a team, raising kids, building a creative project, or just trying to get through the week with fewer loose ends. They come here because they want to try something useful today, not learn theory.

  • Small business owners and creative freelancers
  • Parents, managers, teachers
  • Anyone with too many browser tabs open who wants one of them to actually help

What you'll find here

Every Idea Kitchen piece helps someone make, decide, plan, organize, write, learn, or understand something. No AI content just because a tool is new — only when there's a real-life use for it.

  • Recipes — step-by-step guides for using AI to solve real-life problems
  • What's Simmering — roundups of emerging AI use cases worth watching
  • AI at Work — real people using AI in their jobs
  • AI at Home — AI for family, routines, travel, personal projects

Who's cooking

Hi, I'm Marissa Goldberg — founder of Remote Work Prep and a longtime advocate for working smarter, not longer. I've spent years helping professionals navigate the future of work.

Idea Kitchen is where I share the AI tools and workflows that actually make a difference in my day — and might in yours too. No hype, no doom, no impatient expert rolling their eyes at beginners. Just one useful recipe at a time.
