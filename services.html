Questions you might have

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. Everything I teach is for people who use a computer for work, not people who write code. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this. The whole point is to make AI useful without making it complicated.

Which AI tools does this cover?

The ones you already have or could start using today, like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, plus the tools that plug into your actual work. I'm not here to sell you on any single tool. I help you figure out which ones fit how you work and skip the ones that don't.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

You're exactly who this is built for. Most AI advice assumes you already know the lingo. I start from "I've barely touched this" and take you to "I use it every day." No catching up required.

What's the difference between Chef and Chef's Selection?

Chef gives you everything: the full recipe archive, prompt library, recordings, and live events. Chef's Selection adds a personal layer on top, with a recipe book hand-picked for your tools and role, plus a personal Loom walking you through exactly where to start. Chef's Selection isn't officially open yet, but you can join the waitlist now.

What happens when I book a coaching session?

You pick a time, tell me what you're stuck on ahead of the call, and we spend the hour working through it together. You leave with a clear plan you can act on. No prep or homework required on your end.

Can I expense any of this?

Yes. Most companies have a professional development or learning budget, and AI skills are an easy yes right now. I've put together a ready-to-send email template you can forward to your manager. Fill in two blanks and you're done.

Questions you might have

How is the Team Assessment different from a technical audit?

A technical audit looks at your infrastructure and code. I look at how your team actually works: the meetings, the reports, the recurring tasks that eat hours every week. Then I show you where AI plugs into those processes. It's about your day-to-day, not your tech stack.

Do our people need to be technical?

No. Everything is built for people who use a computer for work, not engineers. Whether your team is AI-curious or AI-skeptical, I meet them where they are and keep it practical.

Which tools do you cover?

The ones your team already uses. I tailor every session and assessment to your actual stack, so the examples are things your people can use the next morning, not generic demos.

What does the Monthly AI Update actually include?

Once a month I bring the three updates that matter for your team's tools, demo what's newly possible, and answer questions live. It's 60 minutes that saves your team from trying to track a fast-moving space on their own.

How many people can join a session?

The Monthly AI Update works for teams of any size. Group coaching is capped small (up to three) so it stays hands-on. Need something bigger or a different shape? Reach out and we'll find the right fit.

How do we get started?

Most teams start with an Assessment so we know where the real opportunities are, then layer on training or a monthly cadence from there. If you already know what you need, we can skip straight to it.

Not sure which option fits?

Tell me a little about where you are and what you're hoping to do with AI, and I'll point you to the option that makes the most sense.
