Common questions

What if the assistant gets something wrong?

It’s built to answer only from your documents and to show the section it used, so anyone can check it. When it can’t find something, it says so. Every answer also lands in the question log, where you can flag the ones that miss.

How is this different from Nextdoor or our Facebook group?

Those wait for someone to post and someone else to answer, and nobody checks the answers. Pipit shows what’s happening before anyone asks, and its answers come from your official documents with the source attached.

How will residents know what’s official?

Every answer links to the official document it came from. The site is labeled as an independent resource and sends residents to the HOA or district whenever that’s where the answer lives.

Do we have to replace our official HOA or district website?

No. Pipit sits alongside it. Sterling Ranch Society is independent and links out to the official sources.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. Every community gets a preview link to test with a few residents before anything goes live.
