Privacy Policy

Last updated June 5, 2026

Idea Kitchen ("we," "us") helps busy professionals learn practical ways to use AI. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit ideakitchen.ai, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep this short and plain, the same way we try to keep everything else.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you join a waitlist or email us, you share your email address and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, we automatically collect basic, non-identifying analytics, such as the pages you view, your approximate region, your device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site.

How we use it

  • To add you to a waitlist you asked to join and to let you know when that offering opens.
  • To respond to your questions and inquiries.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic. It sets cookies and collects usage data as described above. You can opt out using Google's browser add-on, and most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings.

Email and the newsletter

Our newsletter is hosted by Substack. If you subscribe, your information is handled under Substack's privacy policy. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Service providers

We rely on a few trusted services to run the site, including hosting providers and the tools named above. They only process information on our behalf and to provide their service.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from a waitlist, at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. To make a request, just email us.

Children

Idea Kitchen is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email hello@remoteworkprep.com and we'll get back to you.
